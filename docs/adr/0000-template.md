# ADR-NNNN: Title

Complete [the ADR-writing checklist](CHECKLIST.md) before acceptance.

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owners:** Name or team
- **Supersedes:** None

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, forces, and relevant product or security
requirements. Keep the context neutral enough that alternatives remain credible.

## Decision

State the chosen direction precisely. Include what the decision covers,
important boundaries, and what it deliberately does not decide.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier, safer, or more consistent.

### Negative

- What cost, constraint, or operational burden is accepted.

### Follow-up

- What implementation, migration, measurement, documentation, or later decision
  is required.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative name

Explain why it was not selected and when it might be reconsidered.

## References

- Link relevant product, architecture, security, testing, roadmap, issue, or
  prior ADR records.
